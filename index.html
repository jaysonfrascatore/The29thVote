      The 29th Vote

      11,111 simulations of the leader vote

      Each run plays out the secret ballot once: every senator votes Schumer or Van Hollen based on what they've said and done, with shared swings so allies tend to move together.

      These are simulations, not predictions. The odds come from sourced factors and the weights listed at the bottom of this tab. Lock any senator to a side and everything recalculates from only the runs where that happened.

      Projected votes since Nov. 11

      Each point is one hourly check: the average number of votes each candidate got across all 11,111 runs, with the shaded bands covering the middle 80% of runs. The dashed line is 29, the majority of 56 needed to win. The chart runs to the vote.

      Chance of winning since Nov. 11

      How often each candidate won the runs at each check. A 28–28 tie would go to another ballot, so it counts as half a win for each. Hover, or tap and drag, to see what changed at each point.

      How many votes Schumer got

      Each bar is how many of the runs ended with that many votes for Schumer. Tap a bar to see a few of those runs.

      Every senator

      How often each senator voted for each candidate across the runs. Open "Why" for the factors and sources behind it, and use S or VH to lock someone to a side.
